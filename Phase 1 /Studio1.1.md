Project Title : Escape
Prompt : Create an image of a deep sea pod from top view sinking into darker water. Use sharp lines to indicate the pod’s falling speed. The pod is falling towards a giant squid, use irregular lines for the squid’s tentacles subtly wrapping around the pod. Make the two main shapes in the image the metallic oval pod and the sleek giant squid to insinuate threat and aversion to each other.
floating in between a lighter portion and darker portion of water. Put a light source near the deep sea pod, to create further contrast between the giant squid and the man made vessel.
