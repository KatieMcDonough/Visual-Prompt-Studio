Art prompt 1.2 - Acoustic Folk / Americana

Create an image of a young deer sniffing the bones of a dead deer in a vast forest. Light is filtering in from in between the trees, creating a soft light on the deer, while the rest of the forest is darker, creating an emphasis on the contrast between life and death while still feeling peaceful. To create depth in the forest make the trees become more blurry the further away they are from the focal point, the deer. The alive deer next to the dead deer bones represents the passage of time. Keep the trees all relatively similar to promote cohesion in the piece and promote visual interest in the deer. Keep the visual weight mostly symmetrical to promote the peace of nature in the work.


Vocab breakdown

Value- The darks and lights in a piece

Space- Where items are placed in a piece

Unity- How well parts of a piece work together

Balance- An equal distribution of focal points in a piece

Rhythm- How easily the eye can travel across a piece.
