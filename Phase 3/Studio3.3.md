Identity & Self-Expression: Embracing diverse lived experiences

Create a promotional landing page for an interactive exhibit titled “Order and Diversity: The contrast between Greek Idealism to Surrealism”. The subject matter should be Greek marble statues, like Laocoön and His Sons of idealized bodies with contour lines and people that gradually transition into Surrealist versions of themselves inspired by The Son of Man (1964) by René Magritte. All of the statues change into unique forms, representing the modern trend of embracing differences through self expression, emphasising that differences should be celebrated. To emphasize the contrast between the two art forms, the background of the Greek statues will transition with the statues from a plain greyscale background with limited detail to a more unique background that matches the more colorful and creative style of Surrealism. The statues and background will transition horizontally with implied lines, with multiple statues in between to demonstrate the gradual transition.
